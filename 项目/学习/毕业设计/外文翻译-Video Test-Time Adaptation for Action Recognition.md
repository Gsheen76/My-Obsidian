**Video Test-Time Adaptation for Action Recognition 视频测试时自适应动作识别**

# Abstract 摘要

Although action recognition systems can achieve top performance when evaluated on in-distribution test points, they are vulnerable to unanticipated distribution shifts in test data. However, test-time adaptation of video action recognition models against common distribution shifts has so far not been demonstrated. We propose to address this problem with an approach tailored to spatio-temporal models that is capable of adaptation on a single video sample at a step. It consists in a feature distribution alignment technique that aligns online estimates of test set statistics towards the training statistics. We further enforce prediction consistency over temporally augmented views of the same test video sample. Evaluations on three benchmark action recognition datasets show that our proposed technique is architecture-agnostic and able to signiﬁcantly boost the performance on both, the state of the art convolutional architecture TANet and the Video Swin Transformer. Our proposed method demonstrates a substantial performance gain over existing test-time adaptation approaches in both evaluations of a single distribution shift and the challenging case of random distribution shifts. Code will be available at [https://github.com/wlin-at/ViTTA](https://github.com/wlin-at/ViTTA).

尽管动作识别系统在分布内测试点上进行评估时可以达到顶尖性能，但它们在面对测试数据中不可预见的分布变化时却很脆弱。然而，迄今为止尚未证明视频动作识别模型能够针对常见的分布变化进行测试时自适应。我们提出了一种针对时空模型量身定制的方法来解决这个问题，该方法能够在每一步对单个视频样本进行自适应。它包含一种特征分布对齐技术，该技术将测试集统计量的在线估计值与训练统计量对齐。我们进一步强制对同一测试视频样本的时间增强视图进行预测一致性。在三个基准动作识别数据集上的评估表明，我们提出的技术与架构无关，并且能够显著提升最先进的卷积架构TANet和Video Swin Transformer的性能。我们提出的方法在单一分布变化和随机分布变化的挑战性情况的评估中，都比现有的测试时自适应方法展现出显著的性能提升。代码将在[https://github.com/wlin-at/ViTTA](https://github.com/wlin-at/ViTTA)提供。

# 1. Introduction 引言

State-of-the-art neural architectures [8,40,46,48–50] are very effective in action recognition, but recent work shows they are not robust to shifts in the distribution of the test data [33,51]. Unfortunately, in practical scenarios, such distribution shifts are very difﬁcult to avoid, or account for. For example, cameras used for recognizing motorized or pedestrian trafﬁc events may register rare weather conditions, like a hailstorm, and sports action recognition systems can be affected by perturbations generated by spectators at sports arenas, such as the smoke of ﬂares. Shifts in the data distribution can also result from inconspicuous changes in the video processing setup, for instance, a change of the algorithm used to compress the video feed.

最先进的神经架构[8,40,46,48–50]在动作识别方面非常有效，但最近的研究表明它们对测试数据的分布变化[33,51]并不鲁棒。不幸的是，在实际场景中，这种分布变化很难避免或加以考虑。例如，用于识别机动车或行人交通事件的摄像头可能会遇到罕见的恶劣天气条件，如冰雹，而体育动作识别系统可能会受到体育场观众产生的干扰的影响，例如信号弹产生的烟雾。数据分布的变化也可能源于视频处理设置中不显眼的改变，例如，改变用于压缩视频流的算法。

In image classiﬁcation, distribution shift can be mitigated by Test-Time-Adaptation (TTA) [16, 19, 21, 23, 28, 34, 38, 42], which uses the unlabeled test data to adapt the model to the change in data distribution. However, methods developed for image classiﬁcation are not well suited for action recognition. Most action recognition applications require running memory- and computation-hungry temporal models online, with minimal delay, and under tight hardware constraints. Moreover, videos are more vulnerable to distribution shifts than images [2,33,51]. Some examples of such distribution shifts are given in Fig. 1. Due to limited exposure times, video frames are likely to feature higher variations of noise level, provoked by illumination changes. They are more affected by motion blur, which varies with the speed of motion observed in the scene. They also feature stronger compression artifacts, which change with the compression ratio, often dynamically adjusted to the available bandwidth. Our experiments show that existing TTA algorithms, developed for image classiﬁcation, do not cope with these challenges well, yielding marginal improvement over networks used on corrupted data without any adaptation.

在图像分类中，测试时自适应（TTA）[16, 19, 21, 23, 28, 34, 38, 42]可以缓解分布偏移问题，它利用未标记的测试数据来使模型适应数据分布的变化。然而，为图像分类开发的方法并不适用于动作识别。大多数动作识别应用需要在线运行内存和计算密集型的时间模型，要求延迟最小，并在严格的硬件约束下运行。此外，视频比图像更容易受到分布偏移的影响[2,33,51]。图1展示了一些这种分布偏移的例子。由于曝光时间有限，视频帧很可能出现由光照变化引起的更高水平的噪声变化。它们更容易受到运动模糊的影响，运动模糊会随着场景中观察到的运动速度而变化。它们还具有更强的压缩伪影，这会随着压缩比而变化，压缩比通常会根据可用带宽动态调整。我们的实验表明，为图像分类开发的现有TTA算法难以应对这些挑战，与在没有自适应的情况下对损坏数据使用的网络相比，改进效果甚微。

Our goal is to propose an effective method for online test-time-adaptation of action recognition models. Operating online and with small latency can require drastically constraining the batch size, especially when the hardware resources are limited and the employed model is large. We therefore focus on the scenario in which test samples are processed individually, one at a time. To ease the integration of our method in existing systems, we require it to be capable of adapting pretrained networks, both convolutional and transformer-based, without the need to retrain them.

我们的目标是提出一种有效的方法用于动作识别模型的在线测试时自适应。在线操作且具有低延迟可能需要急剧地约束批处理大小，尤其是在硬件资源有限且所用模型较大的情况下。因此，我们专注于测试样本被单独处理、一次一个的场景。为了便于我们将本方法集成到现有系统中，我们要求它能够自适应预训练网络，包括卷积网络和基于 Transformer 的网络，而无需重新训练它们。

In the work, we propose the ﬁrst video test-time adaptation approach - ViTTA. To address the above requirements, we turn to feature alignment [19, 28, 34, 37, 53], a common TTA method that aligns distributions of features computed for test and training data by minimizing discrepancy between their statistics. Feature alignment does not require any modiﬁcations to the training procedure and is architecture-agnostic. However, existing feature alignment methods are not well suited for online adaptation, because they require relatively large test batches to accurately estimate the statistics. We address this by employing the exponential moving average to estimate test feature statistics online. This enables us to perform the alignment by processing one video sample at a time, at a low computational and memory cost. Additionally, we show that even though the temporal dimension of video data poses challenges, it also has a silver lining. We leverage this by creating augmented views of the input videos via temporally resampling frames in the video. This has two beneﬁts: First, multiple augmented views lead to more accurate statistics of the overall video content. Second, it allows us to enforce prediction consistency across the views, making the adaptation more effective.

在本工作中，我们提出了首个视频测试时域自适应方法——ViTTA。为了满足上述要求，我们转向特征对齐 [19, 28, 34, 37, 53]，这是一种常见的TTA方法，通过最小化测试数据和训练数据计算出的特征统计量之间的差异来对齐它们的分布。特征对齐不需要对训练过程进行任何修改，并且与模型架构无关。然而，现有的特征对齐方法并不适合在线自适应，因为它们需要相对较大的测试批次来准确估计统计量。我们通过使用指数移动平均来在线估计测试特征统计量来解决这个问题。这使得我们能够以低计算和内存成本，一次处理一个视频样本来进行对齐。此外，我们表明，尽管视频数据的时域维度带来了挑战，但它也有一线希望。我们通过在视频中对帧进行时间重采样来创建输入视频的增强视图，从而利用这一点。这有两个好处：首先，多个增强视图可以更准确地统计整体视频内容。其次，它允许我们在视图之间强制执行预测一致性，从而使自适应更有效。

Our extensive evaluations on three most popular action recognition benchmarks demonstrate that ViTTA boosts the performance of both TANet [27], the state-of-the-art convolutional architecture, and the Video Swin Transformer [26], and outperforms the existing TTA methods proposed for image data by a signiﬁcant margin. ViTTA performs favorably in both, evaluations of single distribution shift and the challenging case of random distribution shift.

我们在三个最流行的动作识别基准上的广泛评估表明，ViTTA 提升了最先进的卷积架构 TANet [27] 和 Video Swin Transformer [26] 的性能，并且显著优于为图像数据提出的现有 TTA 方法。ViTTA 在单分布偏移评估和随机分布偏移这一挑战性场景中均表现良好。

ViTTA also has a high practical valor. It is fully online and applicable in use cases requiring minimal delays. It does not require collection or storage of test video dataset, which is signiﬁcant in terms of data privacy protection, especially in processing conﬁdential user videos. ViTTA can be seamlessly incorporated in systems already in operation, as it has no requirement of re-training existing networks. Therefore it can harness state-of-the-art video architectures.

ViTTA也具有很高的实用价值。它是完全在线的，适用于需要最小延迟的应用场景。它不需要收集或存储测试视频数据集，这在数据隐私保护方面具有重要意义，尤其是在处理机密用户视频时。ViTTA可以无缝地集成到已运行的系统中，因为它不需要重新训练现有的网络。因此，它可以利用最先进的视频架构。

# 2. Related Work 相关工作

Action Recognition is addressed mainly with CNNbased and transformer-based architectures. CNN-based architectures typically use 3D convolutions, such as C3D [39], I3D [5], Slowfast [9] and X3D [8]. Recent works also deploy 2D convolution with temporal modules [20, 22, 25, 27, 32, 43, 44] to reduce the computational overhead. TEINet [25] learns the temporal features by decoupling the modeling of channel correlation and temporal interaction for efﬁcient temporal modeling. TANet [27] is a 2D CNN with integrated temporal adaptive modules which generates temporal kernels from its own feature maps, achieving state-of-the-art performance among convolutional competitors. Transformer-based models have also been applied for video recognition [1, 3, 7, 26, 30, 31, 54].

动作识别主要通过基于CNN和基于Transformer的架构来解决。基于CNN的架构通常使用3D卷积，例如C3D [39]、I3D [5]、Slowfast [9]和X3D [8]。近期研究也部署了2D卷积与时序模块 [20, 22, 25, 27, 32, 43, 44] 来降低计算开销。TEINet [25] 通过解耦通道相关性和时序交互的建模来学习时序特征，以实现高效的时序建模。TANet [27] 是一个集成了时序自适应模块的2D CNN，它从自身的特征图中生成时序核，在卷积竞争者中取得了最先进的性能。基于Transformer的模型也已应用于视频识别 [1, 3, 7, 26, 30, 31, 54]。

ViViT [1] adds several temporal transformer encoders on the top of spatial encoders. Video swin transformer [26] uses spatio-temporal local windows to compute the selfattention. In this work, we evaluate our adaptation method on TANet and Video Swin Transformer.

ViViT [1] 在空间编码器之上增加了几个时间Transformer编码器。Video Swin Transformer [26] 使用时空局部窗口来计算自注意力。在本工作中，我们评估了我们提出的TANet和Video Swin Transformer的自适应方法。

Robustness of Video Models for action recognition against common corruptions has recently been analyzed [33, 51]. Yi et al. [51] and Schiappa et al. [33] benchmark robustness of common convolutional- and transformer-based spatio-temporal architectures, against several corruptions in video acquisition and video processing. In this work, we perform evaluations on 12 corruptions proposed in these two benchmark works. These corruptions cover various types of noise and digital errors, blur effects of cameras, weather conditions, as well as quality degradation in image and video compression.

近期，视频模型在动作识别方面对常见腐蚀的鲁棒性得到了分析 [33, 51]。Yi等人[51]和Schiappa等人[33]对基于卷积和Transformer的常见时空架构在视频采集和视频处理中的多种腐蚀下的鲁棒性进行了基准测试。在本研究中，我们对这两个基准工作中提出的12种腐蚀进行了评估。这些腐蚀涵盖了各种类型的噪声和数字错误、相机模糊效应、天气条件，以及图像和视频压缩中的质量下降。

Test-Time Adaptation tackles the adaptation to unknown distribution shifts encountered at test-time in an unsupervised manner. It has recently gained increasing attention in the image domain [4,10,17,21,23,28,34,38,42,55]. These approaches can be divided into two distinct groups: The ﬁrst group modiﬁes the training procedure and employs a self-supervised auxiliary task to adapt to distribution shifts at test-time. Sun et al. [38] train the network jointly for self-supervised rotation prediction [12] and the main task of image classiﬁcation. At test-time they adapt to the outof-distribution test data by updating the encoder through the gradients obtained from the auxiliary task of rotation prediction on the test samples. TTT++ [23] propose selfsupervised contrastive learning [6] as an auxiliary objective for adaptation and also aligns the source and target domain feature responses. Recently, Gandelsman et al. [10] use the self-supervised reconstruction task through masked autoencoders [14] for test-time adaptation.

测试时自适应（Test-Time Adaptation）以无监督的方式解决在测试时遇到的未知分布偏移的自适应问题。它最近在图像领域获得了越来越多的关注 [4,10,17,21,23,28,34,38,42,55]。这些方法可以分为两类：第一类修改训练过程，并采用自监督辅助任务来适应测试时的分布偏移。Sun 等人 [38] 联合训练网络以进行自监督旋转预测 [12] 和图像分类的主任务。在测试时，他们通过在测试样本上进行旋转预测的辅助任务获得的梯度来更新编码器，从而适应分布外测试数据。TTT++ [23] 提出将自监督对比学习 [6] 作为自适应的辅助目标，并对齐源域和目标域的特征响应。最近，Gandelsman 等人 [10] 使用了通过掩码自编码器 [14] 进行的自监督重建任务来进行测试时自适应。

The other group of methods, more closely related to our work, propose to adapt off-the-shelf pre-trained networks without altering the training. These methods typically employ post-hoc regularization. TENT [42] adapts a pretrained network at test-time by minimizing the entropy from the output softmax distribution. Similarly, MEMO [55] proposes to adapt the network at test-time by minimizing the entropy of the marginal output distribution across augmentations. SHOT [21] also uses entropy minimization and adds information maximization regularization at test-time. On the other hand, some methods do not update the parameters for the network and instead propose gradient-free approaches for test-time adaptation. For example, LAME [4] only adapts the outputs of the network by using Laplacian regularization and guarantees convergence through a concave-convex optimization procedure. T3A [17] generates pseudo-prototypes from the test samples and replaces the classiﬁer learned on the training set. DUA [28] and NORM [34] only update the statistics of the batch normalization layer for adaptation at test-time.

另一类与我们的工作更相关的方法提出在不改变训练的情况下调整现成的预训练网络。这些方法通常采用事后正则化。TENT [42] 通过最小化输出 softmax 分布的熵来在测试时调整预训练网络。类似地，MEMO [55] 提出通过最小化跨增强的边际输出分布的熵来在测试时调整网络。SHOT [21] 也使用熵最小化，并在测试时添加信息最大化正则化。另一方面，一些方法不更新网络的参数，而是提出用于测试时适应的无梯度方法。例如，LAME [4] 仅通过使用拉普拉斯正则化来调整网络的输出，并通过凹凸优化过程保证收敛。T3A [17] 从测试样本生成伪原型，并替换在训练集上学习到的分类器。DUA [28] 和 NORM [34] 仅在测试时更新批归一化层的统计数据以进行适应。

Despite the intensive development in the image domain, test-time adaptation on video action recognition models, to the best of our knowledge, has not been demonstrated so far. In this work, we propose a video-tailored adaptation method - ViTTA that adapts spatio-temporal models against common distribution shifts on video sequences. ViTTA consists in a feature distribution alignment technique that aligns training statistics with the online estimates of test set statistics. It is model-agnostic and can adapt to both convolutional and transformer-based network without the need to re-train them. We compare ViTTA to existing TTA approaches that do not require to alter the training of source model, and adapt to off-the-shelf pretrained-models.

尽管在图像领域进行了深入研究，但据我们所知，视频动作识别模型上的测试时域自适应尚未得到验证。在本文中，我们提出了一种为视频定制的自适应方法——ViTTA，该方法可适应视频序列中常见的分布偏移。ViTTA 包括一种特征分布对齐技术，该技术将训练统计量与测试集统计量的在线估计值进行对齐。它与模型无关，并且可以适应基于卷积和基于 Transformer 的网络，而无需重新训练它们。我们将 ViTTA 与现有的不需要更改源模型训练的 TTA 方法进行了比较，并使其适应现成的预训练模型。

# 3. Video Test-Time Adaptation (ViTTA) 视频测试时域自适应

We are given a multi-layer neural network ϕ trained in action recognition on a training set of video sequences S , and its optimal parameter vector ˆ θ, resulted from this training. At test time, the network is exposed to unlabeled videos from the test set T , that may be distributed differently than the data in S . Our goal is to adapt ϕ to this distribution shift to maximize its performance on the test videos. The pipeline of our method - ViTTA is shown in Fig. 2.

# 4. Experiments 实验

### 4.1. A Benchmark for Video Test-Time Adaptation 视频测试时域自适应基准

#### 4.1.1 Video datasets. 视频数据集。

We conduct experiments on three action recognition benchmark datasets: UCF101 [35], Something-something v2 (SSv2) [13] and Kinetics 400 (K400) [18]. The UCF101 dataset contains 13320 videos collected from YouTube with 101 action classes. We evaluate on split 1, which consists of 9537 training videos and 3783 validation videos. SSv2 is a large-scale action dataset with 168K training videos and 24K validation videos, comprised of 174 classes. K400 is the most popular benchmark for action recognition tasks, containing around 240K training videos and 20K validation videos in 400 classes.

我们在三个动作识别基准数据集上进行实验：UCF101 [35]、Something-something v2 (SSv2) [13] 和 Kinetics 400 (K400) [18]。UCF101数据集包含13320个从YouTube收集的视频，涵盖101个动作类别。我们在split 1上进行评估，该split包含9537个训练视频和3783个验证视频。SSv2是一个大规模动作数据集，包含168K个训练视频和24K个验证视频，共计174个类别。K400是动作识别任务中最流行的数据集，包含约240K个训练视频和20K个验证视频，共计400个类别。

#### 4.1.2 Corruptions. 损坏。

We evaluate on 12 corruption types proposed in [33,51] that benchmark robustness of spatio-temporal models. These 12 corruptions are: Gaussian noise, pepper noise, salt noise, shot noise, zoom blur, impulse noise, defocus blur, motion blur, jpeg compression, contrast, rain, H.265 ABR compression. They cover various types of noise and digital errors, blur effects of cameras, weather conditions, as well as quality degradation in image and video compression.

我们在 [33,51] 提出的 12 种腐蚀类型上进行评估，以评测时空模型的鲁棒性。这 12 种腐蚀类型包括：高斯噪声, 椒盐噪声, 盐噪声, 散粒噪声, 缩放模糊, 脉冲噪声, 散焦模糊, 运动模糊, JPEG 压缩, 对比度, 雨, H.265 ABR 压缩.这 12 种腐蚀类型包括：它们涵盖了各种类型的噪声和数字错误、相机的模糊效应、天气条件以及图像和视频压缩中的质量下降。

Following [33, 51], we evaluate on the validation sets of the three datasets. We use the implementation from these two benchmark papers for corruption generation. As the robustness analysis indicates approximate linear correlation between severity level and the performance drop, We evaluate on the corruptions of the most severe case at level 5.

遵循[33, 51]，我们在三个数据集的验证集上进行评估。我们使用这两个基准论文中的实现来生成腐蚀。由于鲁棒性分析表明严重性级别与性能下降之间存在近似线性相关性，因此我们在最严重情况（级别 5）的腐蚀上进行评估。

### 4.2. Implementation Details 实现细节

We evaluate our approach on two model architectures: TANet [27] based on ResNet50 [15], and Video Swin Transformer [26] based on Swin-B [24]. TANet is the state-of-the-art convolutional network for action recognition and Video Swin Transformer is adapted from Swin Transformer [24]. More model speciﬁcs are given in supplementary.

我们在两种模型架构上评估了我们的方法：基于ResNet50 [27] 的TANet [15]，以及基于Swin-B [26] 的Video Swin Transformer [24]。TANet是用于动作识别的最新卷积网络，Video Swin Transformer 是从Swin Transformer [24] 适配而来。更多模型细节请参见补充材料。

We perform distribution alignment of features from the normalization layers in the last two blocks. We set the batch size to 1 for evaluation on all datasets. We adapt to each video only once. Following common practice in online testtime adaptation [4, 38, 45], we perform inference right after adapting to a sample and report the accumulated accuracy for all samples. On TANet, we set the learning rate to 5e − 5 for UCF and 1e − 5 for SSv2 and K400. On Video Swin Transformer, learning rate is set to 1e − 5 for all datasets. For the temporal augmentation, we perform uniform equidistant sampling and random spatial cropping.

我们对最后两个块的归一化层的特征进行分布对齐。我们在所有数据集的评估中将批大小设置为 1。我们仅对每个视频进行一次适应。遵循在线测试时自适应中的常见做法 [4, 38, 45]，我们在适应样本后立即进行推理，并报告所有样本的累积准确率。在 TANet 上，我们将 UCF 的学习率设置为 5e − 5，将 SSv2 和 K400 的学习率设置为 1e − 5。在 Video Swin Transformer 上，所有数据集的学习率均设置为 1e − 5。对于时间增强，我们执行均匀等距采样和随机空间裁剪。

我们将我们的自适应算法与以下基线进行评估，这些基线在不改变训练条件的情况下适应现成的预训练模型。SourceOnly 表示直接使用在训练数据上训练的模型进行预测，无需自适应。NORM [34] 包括将批归一化层的统计量自适应到测试数据。DUA [28] 在线自适应批归一化层。TENT [42] 通过最小化测试预测的熵来学习特征图的仿射变换。SHOT (online) [21] 在最小化单个预测熵的同时，最大化预测类别批分布的熵。T3A [16] 从测试数据构建伪原型。

### 4.4. Ablation studies 消融研究

#### 4.4.1 The choice of feature maps to align 选择用于对齐的特征图

Aligning the distribution of the ultimate feature map of a deep network is a common practice in TTA, and in the broader ﬁeld of domain adaptation [11, 19, 36, 53]. On the other hand, recent work hints that adapting multiple layers in the network [28, 34, 52] might be a more powerful technique, at least when the adaptation is limited to statistics of the batch normalization layers. To verify which of these approaches fares better in our task, we run a series of experiments in which we adapt the TANet and Video Swin transformer to the corrupted UCF101 validation sets. We apply the alignment to the feature map outputs from different combinations of blocks. Here block refers to either convolutional bottleneck in TANet or stage in Swin transformer. We repeat these experiments for four adaptation variants: either by the last block of the architecture, or by the last two, three, or all four blocks. The results, presented in Table 4, suggest that an intermediate approach: aligning feature maps produced by the last two blocks, performs best. We attribute this to the fact that leaving too many degrees of freedom during adaptation might lead to matching the distribution of the last layers, but without transferring feature semantics. On the other hand, some degree of freedom might be needed in the lower layers of the architecture for the network to learn to map appearance of the corrupted data to the feature space of the layers further in the computation graph, learned on the training data without corruption.

在测试时域自适应（TTA）以及更广泛的域自适应领域[11, 19, 36, 53]中，使深度网络的最终特征图的分布对齐是一种常见做法。另一方面，近期研究表明，使网络中的多个层进行自适应[28, 34, 52]可能是一种更强大的技术，至少当自适应仅限于批归一化层的统计时。为了验证哪种方法在我们的任务中表现更好，我们进行了一系列实验，在这些实验中，我们将TANet和Video Swin transformer适配到损坏的UCF101验证集上。我们将对齐应用于来自不同块组合的特征图输出。这里的块（block）指的是TANet中的卷积瓶颈或Swin transformer中的阶段（stage）。我们对四种自适应变体重复了这些实验：要么通过架构的最后一个块，要么通过最后两个、三个或所有四个块。结果如表4所示，表明一种中间方法：对齐由最后两个块产生的特征图，表现最佳。我们认为这是因为在自适应过程中留下过多的自由度可能导致匹配最后一层的分布，但未能转移特征语义。另一方面，架构的较低层可能需要一定程度的自由度，以便网络学习将损坏数据的外观映射到计算图中更深层的特征空间，而这些特征空间是在没有损坏的训练数据上学习到的。


Table 2. Top-1 Classiﬁcation Accuracy (%) for all corruptions in the UCF101 dataset, while using the TANet backbone. DUA is evaluated with batch size of 1, following the setting in the original work [28]. All the other methods are evaluated with batch size of 8.

表 2. 在 UCF101 数据集上，使用 TANet 主干网络时，所有腐蚀情况下的 Top-1 分类准确率 (%)。DUA 遵循原始论文 [28] 中的设置，使用批量大小 1 进行评估。所有其他方法均使用批量大小 8 进行评估。

Table 3. Mean Top-1 Classiﬁcation Accuracy (%) with random distribution shifts. For each video in the test set, we randomly select 1 out of 13 distribution shifts (12 corruption types + original test set). We run these experiments 3 times while shufﬂing the order of the videos and report the average results.

表 3. 随机分布偏移下的平均 Top-1 分类准确率 (%)。对于测试集中的每个视频，我们从 13 种分布偏移（12 种损坏类型 + 原始测试集）中随机选择 1 种。我们对视频顺序进行洗牌并运行这些实验 3 次，然后报告平均结果。

Table 4. Mean Top-1 Classiﬁcation Accuracy (%) over corruptions. We adapt TANet and Video swin tansformer on UCF101 by aligning feature maps from different blocks. block refers to either convolutional bottleneck in TANet or stage in Swin transformer.

表 4. 腐蚀上的平均 Top-1 分类准确率 (%)。我们通过对齐来自不同块的特征图，将 TANet 和 Video Swin Transformer 应用于 UCF101。块（block）是指 TANet 中的卷积瓶颈或 Swin Transformer 中的阶段。

#### 4.4.2 Statistics stored in normalization layers 归一化层中存储的统计信息

For feature distribution alignment, our method requires feature means and variances computed on the training data.

为了特征分布对齐，我们的方法需要使用在训练数据上计算得到的特征均值和方差。

We use the TANet backbone for these experiments. BNS refers to the statistics stored in the batch normalization layers. Src − Computed refers to the statistics which we calculate from the source data for distribution alignment.

表 5. 在 UCF101 数据集上，使用不同种类的源统计数据进行对齐时的平均 Top-1 分类准确率（%）。我们在此实验中使用了 TANet 主干网络。BNS 指的是存储在批归一化层中的统计数据。Src − Computed 指的是我们为分布对齐而从源数据计算出的统计数据。

When training data is no longer available, these statistics could be computed on other data with the similar distribution. For architectures that contain batch normalization layers, using the running means and variances, accumulated at these layers during training, represents a convenient alternative. However, these statistics might misrepresent true means and variances of the data, as reported by Wu et al. [47]. To investigate how their inaccuracy affects performance, we compare the results attained by using them as adaptation targets to those obtained by using statistics computed on the training set. We present the results in Tab 5. They conﬁrm that relying on the running mean and variance yields performance slightly lower than computing the statistics from scratch, but still higher than that of the baselines. This demonstrates that our ViTTA can also only rely on batch norm statistics in architectures with BN layers, at a modest performance penalty.

当训练数据不再可用时，这些统计量可以基于具有相似分布的其他数据进行计算。对于包含批归一化层的架构，使用在训练过程中于这些层累积的运行均值和方差是方便的替代方案。然而，如Wu等人[47]所述，这些统计量可能无法准确代表数据的真实均值和方差。为了探究其不准确性对性能的影响，我们将使用它们作为适应目标的所得结果与使用训练集上计算的统计量所得结果进行比较。我们将结果呈现在表5中。它们证实，依赖运行均值和方差所获得的性能略低于从头计算统计量，但仍高于基线性能。这表明，在具有BN层的架构中，我们的ViTTA也可以仅依赖批归一化统计量，尽管性能会略有下降。

#### 4.4.3 Number of views and prediction consistency 观看次数和预测一致性

In temporal augmentation, we compute test statistics on temporally augmented views of the input and enforce prediction consistency among the views. We verify how much performance gain these design choices yield in Table 6. Without prediction consistency, sampling two temporally augmented views (77.46%) brings around two percent improvement in comparison to only one view (75.57%). Applying the prediction consistency regularization among the two views further adds 0.74% improvement. Sampling more than two augmented views adds little beneﬁt. We set the number of views to two for better adaptation efﬁciency.

在时间增强中，我们计算输入的时间增强视图上的检验统计量，并强制视图之间的一致性预测。我们在表6中验证了这些设计选择带来的性能提升。在没有预测一致性的情况下，采样两个时间增强视图（77.46%）比仅采样一个视图（75.57%）能带来大约2%的性能提升。在两个视图之间应用预测一致性正则化进一步增加了0.74%的性能提升。采样两个以上的增强视图带来的收益很小。为了获得更好的适应效率，我们将视图数量设置为两个。

表 7. 在 UCF101 数据集上，使用 TANet 在所有腐蚀类型下的平均 Top-1 分类准确率（%）。我们对时间增强视图的帧采样策略进行了消融研究。

#### 4.4.4 Temporal sampling strategies 时间采样策略

Our method relies on temporal augmentation to generate multiple views of the input video sequence and enforce consistency of their predictions. Common temporal sampling methods can be found in video recognition literature [5, 8, 39, 44]. We embed the most representative of these techniques in our adaptation algorithm and evaluate the resulting performance. Uniform and dense are common strategies for video segment selection. Random and equidistant are strategies of sampling frames in each video segment. total random refers to completely randomly sampling frames from the entire video.

我们的方法依赖于时间增强来生成输入视频序列的多个视图，并强制执行其预测的一致性。在视频识别文献 [5, 8, 39, 44] 中可以找到常见的时间采样方法。我们将这些技术中最具代表性的嵌入到我们的适应算法中，并评估由此产生的性能。均匀和密集是视频片段选择的常用策略。随机和等距是每个视频片段中采样帧的策略。total random 指的是从整个视频中完全随机地采样帧。

The results in Table 7 show that our ViTTA generalizes well to different types of frame sampling strategies, as they all demonstrate clear performance boost in comparison to the case of one view (75.57%). The uniform-equidistant approach perform best. We hypothesize that this stems from the fact that this sampling technique keeps the interval between the video frames constant, while yielding frame sequences that span the entire video sequence, which leads to more accurate statistics of the overall video content.

表7的结果表明，我们的ViTTA能够很好地泛化到不同类型的帧采样策略，因为与单视图情况（75.57%）相比，所有这些策略都显示出明显的性能提升。均匀等距方法表现最佳。我们假设这是因为该采样技术保持了视频帧之间恒定的时间间隔，同时产生了跨越整个视频序列的帧序列，从而能够更准确地统计整体视频内容。

#### 4.4.5 Continuous adaptation 持续适应

We check the capacity of the methods to re-adapt to the uncorrupted data. We perform an experiment in the continuous adaptation scenario. When sequentially feeding the test data, we periodically switch the corruption ‘on’ and ‘off’ around every ﬁve hundred test videos. We use the Gaussian noise as the corruption technique.

我们检查了该方法重新适应未损坏数据的能力。我们在连续适应场景中进行了一项实验。在顺序输入测试数据时，我们大约每五百个测试视频切换一次损坏的开启和关闭。我们使用高斯噪声作为损坏技术。


As seen in Fig. 3, ViTTA performs constantly the best on corrupted periods. Both our approach and DUA can recover the original performance on the un-corrupted periods, while TENT performs slightly worse when adapting to the un-corrupted data. DUA only corrects the batch norm statistics without updating model parameters. It keeps the knowledge on the un-corrupted but has limited gain in corrupted periods. TENT updates the model with an entropy minimization loss and has even slightly worse performance than Source-Only in un-corrupted periods. In comparison, our method updates the entire model and still quickly restores the performance in un-corrupted periods, demonstrating the fast reactivity. As our method accumulates the target statistics in an online manner, it also has gradually improved performance when going through the four corrupted periods.

如图 3 所示，ViTTA 在损坏期间表现始终最佳。我们的方法和 DUA 都能在未损坏期间恢复原始性能，而 TENT 在适应未损坏数据时性能略有下降。DUA 仅更正批归一化统计量而不更新模型参数。它保留了在未损坏数据上的知识，但在损坏期间的收益有限。TENT 使用熵最小化损失更新模型，在未损坏期间的性能甚至略差于仅源模型。相比之下，我们的方法更新了整个模型，并且在未损坏期间仍能快速恢复性能，展示了快速的响应能力。由于我们的方法以在线方式累积目标统计量，因此在经历四个损坏期间时，性能也逐渐提高。

# 5. Conclusion 结论

We address the problem of test-time adaptation of video action recognition models against common corruptions, We propose a video-tailored method that aligns the training statistics with the online estimates of target statistics. To further boost the performance, we enforce prediction consistency among temporally augmented views of a video sample. We benchmark existing TTA techniques on three action recognition datasets, with 12 common image- and video-speciﬁc corruptions. Our proposed method ViTTA performs favorably in evaluation of both, single corruption and the challenging random corruption scenario. Furthermore, it demonstrates fast reactivity on adaptation performance, faced with periodic change of distribution shift.

我们解决了视频动作识别模型在常见腐蚀下的测试时自适应问题，我们提出了一种针对视频的方法，将训练统计量与目标统计量的在线估计进行对齐。为了进一步提升性能，我们强制对视频样本的时间增强视图进行预测一致性约束。我们在三个动作识别数据集上对现有的TTA技术进行了基准测试，涵盖了12种常见的图像和视频特定腐蚀。我们提出的ViTTA方法在单腐蚀和具有挑战性的随机腐蚀场景的评估中均表现良好。此外，它在面对周期性分布变化时，展现出对自适应性能的快速响应能力。
